# KGM UAE Microsite — Creative Master Direction

## 1. Creative Intent

The KGM UAE Microsite is a launch experience announcing the arrival of KGM in the UAE.

The approved project scope, information architecture, five-section structure, user journey, bilingual requirements, technical requirements, and content guardrails remain unchanged.

This document defines the creative ambition and visual execution of the approved microsite.

The website must not feel like a generic landing page, dealership website, template, or simple campaign page.

It should feel like a premium automotive manufacturer experience with a cinematic, dramatic, confident, modern, and image-led visual language.

The creative objective is to make users feel that something significant has arrived.

The central idea is:

KGM has arrived in the UAE.

The experience should create desire for the vehicles while making it simple and natural for users to register their interest.

---

## 2. The Creative Feeling

The experience should feel:

- Cinematic
- Premium
- Dramatic
- Confident
- Modern
- Automotive
- Image-led
- Atmospheric
- Sophisticated
- High quality
- Purposeful
- Unforgettable

The visual ambition should be comparable to a premium automotive manufacturer launch experience.

The website should feel carefully art-directed rather than assembled from standard website sections.

Avoid generic card grids, predictable SaaS-style layouts, excessive rounded cards, unnecessary decoration, and ordinary landing-page patterns.

---

## 3. The Approved Microsite Structure Remains

The approved five-section microsite structure remains unchanged:

1. Hero
2. The Models
3. Gallery
4. Showrooms
5. Register Interest

Persistent functionality remains:

- EN / AR language switcher
- Full RTL support for Arabic
- Register Interest CTA
- WhatsApp click-to-chat
- PDPL consent
- Analytics

Do not create additional major sections.

Do not turn the microsite into a full website.

Do not create separate model detail pages.

Do not introduce CMS, CRM integration, news, FAQs, warranty content, accounts, or other excluded functionality.

---

## 4. The Hero — The Arrival

The Hero is the emotional centre of the experience.

It should communicate the arrival of KGM into the UAE without relying on excessive copy.

The hero should feel like a cinematic automotive film.

The existing hero video supplied by the project owner should be considered the primary source material where appropriate.

The visual direction may include:

- UAE desert landscapes
- Dramatic roads
- Coastal and beach environments
- Mountain landscapes where appropriate
- Modern UAE architectural environments where appropriate
- Golden-hour and cinematic lighting
- Powerful vehicle movement
- Convoy or multiple KGM vehicles travelling together
- Wide cinematic compositions
- Dramatic scale
- Atmospheric dust, light, landscape, and motion

The hero should not depend on showing human faces.

The vehicles and the UAE landscape should be the protagonists.

The hero should communicate:

A new automotive presence has arrived.

The visual storytelling should move from anticipation to arrival.

The hero should support the existing ARRIVE stage of the user journey.

Possible minimal messaging direction:

A NEW JOURNEY BEGINS

KGM HAS ARRIVED IN THE UAE

The exact final copy must use approved project content and must not be invented as factual brand claims.

The hero must include a clear path toward exploring the KGM range and registering interest.

---

## 5. The Models — Premium Vehicle Discovery

The Models section remains one of the core approved sections.

The model experience should be presented as a premium automotive discovery experience.

The preferred direction is a large, visually dominant interactive model carousel rather than a basic grid of small cards.

The user should be able to move through the confirmed KGM UAE lineup in a clear, elegant, visually engaging way.

Each model presentation may include:

- Large approved vehicle imagery
- Model name
- Approved category or classification
- Approved short description
- Three approved key numbers when supplied
- Register Interest CTA

The confirmed UAE lineup and final model order must come from approved project information.

Do not invent:

- Model availability
- Specifications
- Performance figures
- Features
- Key numbers
- Claims
- Launch status

If information is not confirmed, use CONTENT REQUIRED / NOT SPECIFIED IN PROJECT MATERIALS.

The model carousel should feel like an automotive product presentation, not a collection of generic website cards.

The visual priority should be:

Vehicle first.

Model name second.

Information third.

Conversion action fourth.

The model experience must support the EXPLORE stage of the journey.

---

## 6. The Gallery — Desire and Trust

The Gallery remains an approved core section.

It should support the emotional world of KGM and the vehicles.

The gallery should feel editorial and cinematic rather than like a basic image grid.

Use approved imagery only.

The visual language may combine:

- Vehicle photography
- UAE environments
- Driving environments
- Detail photography
- Landscape imagery
- Lifestyle context where approved

The gallery should build desire and confidence.

The gallery must not invent imagery or imply unconfirmed claims.

---

## 7. Showrooms — Confidence and Action

The Showrooms section remains an approved core section.

It should feel integrated into the premium automotive experience.

Showroom information must be based on approved project information.

Do not invent:

- Addresses
- Opening hours
- Coordinates
- Locations
- Contact information

If information is unavailable, clearly use CONTENT REQUIRED / NOT SPECIFIED IN PROJECT MATERIALS.

The section should make it easy for users to understand where to go and how to get directions once approved information is supplied.

---

## 8. Register Interest — The Conversion Moment

The Register Interest section remains the primary conversion point.

The form should feel simple, premium, and frictionless.

The approved fields are:

- Name
- Phone
- Emirate
- Model of interest

The experience should communicate:

- What information is required
- Validation errors
- PDPL consent
- Submission progress
- Success
- What happens next

The form must remain short and mobile-friendly.

The design should make registration feel like the natural conclusion of the experience.

The REGISTER stage should feel clear and confident, not aggressive.

---

## 9. Visual Design Principles

The design should be led by:

- Strong visual hierarchy
- Large-scale automotive imagery
- Cinematic composition
- Confident typography
- Controlled motion
- Premium spacing
- Strong contrast
- Editorial rhythm
- Purposeful transitions

Avoid:

- Generic template layouts
- Excessive card-based UI
- Unnecessary rounded containers
- Excessive gradients
- Decorative UI without purpose
- Excessive animation
- Visual clutter
- Overly playful styling
- Standard startup landing-page patterns

The website should feel designed around vehicles and movement.

---

## 10. Brand and Content Integrity

KGM brand guidelines and approved project assets always take priority.

The creative direction must never override brand guidelines.

Use approved:

- Logos
- Colours
- Typography
- Vehicle imagery
- Model information
- Brand language

Do not invent brand claims, specifications, model availability, showroom information, legal text, contact information, or factual marketing statements.

Creative ambition does not permit invented content.

---

## 11. Same Microsite, Higher Creative Ambition

This is not a new project scope.

This is not a separate campaign website.

This is not a full KGM website.

It is the approved KGM UAE Microsite executed with a significantly stronger cinematic automotive creative direction.

The project should preserve:

ARRIVE → EXPLORE → TRUST → REGISTER

while making the experience feel:

KGM has arrived.

The final result should be beautiful enough to create desire and simple enough to create action.
