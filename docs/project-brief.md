## 1. Overview & Purpose (CONFIRMED)

**Proposal title:** "Microsite Quick-Start — An Alternate Approach · Proposal."
**Tagline:** "Live before the first cars land."

A **bilingual, image-led microsite that captures interest from day one, while the
full website is built without time pressure.**

**The rationale (Why this approach):**
- The first KGM vehicles arrive in the UAE in **August 2026**, in a matter of weeks.
- A full website done properly takes months (design system, CMS, CRM, bilingual QA).
  Rushing it to meet the cars would waste what the main proposal gets right.
- The quick-start closes the gap: a premium bilingual microsite, **live before the
  first delivery**, while the full website is built calmly in parallel.

**Three stated benefits:**
1. **Live before the cars arrive** — launch marketing has a premium destination from
   day one instead of a "coming soon" page.
2. **Leads from day one** — every early enquiry is captured and emailed straight to
   the sales team.
3. **The full website, without the rush** — the main site is built properly in parallel.

**Single outcome:** "One page. Everything that matters." — *a registered lead.*

## 5. Bilingual EN / AR Requirements (CONFIRMED)

**"Arabic is a first-class citizen."** Both languages ship together on day one — the
microsite launches EN + AR simultaneously.

- **English — LTR.**
- **Arabic — RTL, fully mirrored.**
- **True mirroring** — layout, navigation, forms and sliders flip, not just the text.
- **Arabic typography** — Cairo typeface, tuned line-height, designed for Arabic.
- **Human translation** — all microsite copy professionally localised EN→AR.
- **Smart switching** — EN | AR toggle **+ hreflang**, so Google serves each audience
  the right version.

> The **final localised Arabic copy** (and the approved English copy it is translated
> from) is **CONTENT REQUIRED** — see §13. Human translation is a delivery activity,
> not something to be machine-generated or invented.

## 9. Register Interest Requirements (CONFIRMED)

**"From interest to inbox in seconds."** Lead workflow: **FILL → VALIDATE → NOTIFY →
FOLLOW UP.**

- **FILL** — fields: **Name · Phone · Emirate · Model of interest.** ~30 seconds, in
  English or Arabic.
- **VALIDATE** — field validation, **spam & bot protection**, **PDPL consent recorded.**
- **NOTIFY** — instant email to the salesperson containing every detail, ready to dial.
- **FOLLOW UP** — sales calls back while the interest is hot.

Supporting lead-capture requirements:
- **WhatsApp click-to-chat** — for buyers who would rather message than fill a form.
- **GA4 events** — every submission tracked so marketing sees which campaign produced
  it (see §11).
- **CRM-ready** — leads structured and **exportable**, so they can be imported cleanly
  into **Zoho or CRAMS** when the full website lands. *(Export-readiness only —
  direct CRM integration is out of scope; see §12.)*
- **No lead lost** — submissions are **also logged in a running sheet**; the inbox is
  never the only copy.

> **CONTENT REQUIRED:** the sales notification email recipient(s); PDPL / consent
> wording; the running-sheet destination (e.g. which spreadsheet/sheet). See §13.
> The only email in the proposal is the agency contact `marwan@worxbyglimpse.com`,
> which is **not** a sales-lead recipient.

## Creative Direction

The KGM UAE Microsite must feel like a **modern automotive manufacturer website, not
a generic campaign landing page.**

The experience should be:

- Premium
- Modern
- Cinematic
- Image-led
- Automotive-focused
- Confident
- High quality
- Mobile-first

The microsite should **create desire for the KGM vehicles while making it extremely
easy for users to register their interest.**

### Reference: Official KGM Website

The official KGM website may be used for **research and inspiration only**, to
understand the brand's automotive context, model ecosystem, and the level of quality
expected of a modern car manufacturer's digital experience:

- **https://en.kg-mobility.com/**

It may inform:

- How vehicle models are presented
- Automotive visual hierarchy
- Model discovery
- Vehicle-focused storytelling
- Premium automotive digital experiences
- Brand confidence

**The KGM UAE Microsite must be an original experience.** Do **not** copy the official
website's:

- Layout
- Page structure
- Written content
- Visual design
- Code
- Interactions
- Assets

The microsite must have its **own unique design direction** while maintaining a
premium automotive-manufacturer standard.

### Creative Priorities (supporting ARRIVE → EXPLORE → TRUST → REGISTER)

1. A powerful **cinematic first impression**.
2. Strong **visual presentation of the confirmed KGM UAE vehicle lineup**.
3. Premium **model discovery without requiring separate model detail pages**.
4. A visually engaging **gallery that builds trust and desire**.
5. Clear **showroom information and directions**.
6. A **frictionless Register Interest experience**.
7. **Persistent but tasteful conversion actions**.
8. A **first-class English and Arabic experience with proper RTL behavior**.

### Content Guardrails (unchanged from §6–§13)

Creative direction does **not** unlock invented content. Do **not** invent:

- Vehicle specifications
- Model availability
- Key numbers
- Vehicle claims
- Brand claims
- Images
- Showroom information
- Contact information
- Legal content

Where a creative concept depends on information that is not yet confirmed, label it
clearly as **CONTENT REQUIRED / NOT SPECIFIED IN PROJECT MATERIALS** and defer to the
items tracked in §13.
